Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. Sample text for layout only; final wording to be supplied by legal.

This policy explains how IKONIC-M collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website, in line with applicable data protection laws, including Singapore's PDPA and Vietnam's personal data regulations.

1. Information we collect

We collect the details you give us when you send an enquiry, request our brochure or subscribe to our newsletter, such as your name, company, email address and phone number. We also collect technical information about how you use this website, including the pages you visit and the device and browser you use.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiries, send the materials you have asked for, share the updates you have subscribed to and improve this website. We do not use your personal data for any other purpose without your consent.

3. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies keep this website working and are always on. Analytics and marketing cookies are used only if you accept them. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie preferences on this website.

4. Sharing and retention

We share personal data only with group companies and service providers who help us run this website and respond to you, and only under agreements that protect your data. We keep your data for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires.
